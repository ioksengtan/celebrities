I don’t think we understand all of the impacts of technology on small children for good or for bad, but as a parent making decisions for my own kids, I will certainly say when I watch kids that are glued to a touchscreen of some sort scrolling through brain rot short form video, I have a strong instinct that that’s a bad thing. [SA-2026-007-Q05 33:44] Sam Altman: I believe the right answer here is to navigate the challenges so that we can get the upsides. And I do not want to stop technological progress. I want that cure for cancer. I want that cure for Alzheimer’s. And I want the kids in 10 years to be doing much more amazing things than self-driving golf carts. But I understand the instinct to say, “I don’t want the change. I don’t want the risk. Just stop it. We’ll deal with our current life.” I really hope that hundreds of years from now, people look back at our lives and just can’t believe how bad we had it. And I think this is the story of civilization and technology and I’m sure it’ll keep going.
