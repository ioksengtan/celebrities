[SA-2026-008-Q01 第 1 段 00:37；SA-2026-008-Q02 第 1 段 01:02] ALTMAN:  AI has to be built for people. It has to be, like, good for people. […] As the models have gotten more capable, the safety cases that we now use have to be held to a higher and higher standard, and the models can do more. So we will have many great new models to come, but what we have said is that alignment, safety, monitoring, security have to stay way ahead of capabilities. And we have had models in the past where we say, this is not quite at the bar we want, and we will make another one and fix it and launch it later.

[SA-2026-008-Q03 第 2 段 00:36] ALTMAN:  I don't think it's a full solution. And I worry that if we treat AI safety as only an engineering problem, we will miss the very important point that we have a science problem in front of us. We still have discovery about how to align these models, and we have to solve that scientific problem too.

[SA-2026-008-Q04 第 2 段 03:56] ALTMAN:  I am not worried about being first with new hardware. New hardware is very hard to do. Like, new computing form factors come along very rarely, and the quality bar that we want to hit for something that you will love and really be a key part of the way you interface with AI, that's going to take a while, and we're going to do that at a high level of quality.

[SA-2026-008-Q05 第 2 段 04:34] ALTMAN:  I mean, I think everyone doing something this important to society deserves great scrutiny. I did see the movie trailer.
