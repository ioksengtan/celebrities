Eric Schmidt: The term that I've been using is being an AI-native CEO. So, what I'm trying to do in all my things is I'm tying to fully automate the execution of the businesses. In the factory floor in Relativity, I was walking through it one day and I thought, how many computers are here? So, I said, why don't we connect them all? So, they just connected all the computers. It occurs as a natural byproduct of getting the digital systems connected. Furthermore, if you take generic data, unstructured data, and you connect it all, these systems are smart enough that they can interpolate between one data normalcy and another.
